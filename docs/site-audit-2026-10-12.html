Growmerce website audit

The core links work. The biggest improvements are clearer page addresses for Google, a complete sitemap, and removing links that lead nowhere.

Checked the live Growmerce and Growsearch sites, 54 requested URLs, redirect chains, page metadata, mobile navigation, and current source. No website code was changed, no server was started, and no forms were submitted.

What is already present

  • HTTPS and working main pages. All 13 listed main marketing pages returned 200. A made-up page correctly returned 404 and noindex.
  • Page titles and descriptions. The marketing pages have distinct titles and descriptions; the homepage has a broad brand description.
  • robots.txt and sitemap.xml. Both exist and return 200. robots.txt blocks /admin and /api/. The sitemap needs corrections below.
  • Analytics and Google verification tags. Google Analytics G-XBS59VYVXV and a Search Console verification tag are present. Account verification, traffic collection, sitemap submission, and actual Google indexing were not checked.
  • Blog article SEO. The published article has a canonical tag, article metadata, share image, Twitter large-image card, and BlogPosting structured data.
  • Mobile basics. The home and Growsearch pages fit a 390px viewport without horizontal overflow; the homepage menu opened. Image alt attributes are present on both homepages. This was a spot check, not a full accessibility audit.
  • Indexing controls for private flows. Affiliate login/signup and the preview page have noindex. Signed-out /admin redirects to login.

Fix first

  1. Make page addresses consistent. growmerce.ai permanently redirects to www.growmerce.ai, but the sitemap, blog canonical tags, and base metadata use the non-www address. Twelve of the 13 main marketing pages have no canonical tag. Add a preferred-address tag per page and align it with the live domain, redirects, sitemap, and internal links. These are signals for Google; the current mismatch does not by itself prove lost rankings.
  2. Resolve duplicate pages on the Growsearch subdomain. /about, /try, /contact, /help, /privacy, /terms, /pricing, and /products/ai-search also return 200 on search.growmerce.ai, mostly without canonical tags. Shared navigation uses relative links, so visitors remain on these copies. Send shared company-page links to the main site and redirect redundant subdomain copies, or explicitly choose their canonical addresses.
  3. Complete the sitemap. The live main sitemap contains 13 marketing URLs but omits the published /blog/ai-search-for-ecommerce article and both Growsearch pages. The subdomain serves the same main-site sitemap and robots.txt points to that sitemap. Give Growsearch appropriate sitemap coverage and ensure publishing updates the article list. All existing sitemap entries use addresses that redirect to www.
  4. Replace six empty footer links. Newsroom, Coming soon, Customer Success, LinkedIn, Instagram, and X all use #. Connect them to real destinations or remove their clickable treatment.
  5. Keep old designs out of search. /v, /v/1, and /v/4 are publicly accessible and have no noindex directive. Add noindex to archived concepts, or remove public access when those previews are no longer needed.

Smaller improvements

  • Share previews: main marketing and Growsearch pages inherit the same Growmerce Open Graph title, description, and homepage URL; no share image was found on those pages. Give each page accurate share metadata and add a branded image. The blog article already has this.
  • Trial copy: the live homepage says 15 days, while the live pricing cards, pricing description, and terms say 14. Confirm the actual Shopify trial and make the public copy agree. Uncommitted source changes already move some copy to 15 days, but that does not establish the configured trial.
  • Affiliate discoverability: the public affiliate program is noindex and omitted from the sitemap because its parent layout excludes the entire affiliate area. Keep account screens excluded; allow the public program page if you want agencies to find it through Google.
  • Structured data: no Organization, WebSite, or SoftwareApplication JSON-LD was found on marketing/product pages. This is an enhancement, not a requirement for indexing; add only accurate information matching visible content.
  • Link labels: “All products” leads to Growsearch features, and the pricing page’s “Compare with competitors” leads to those same features. Rename them or point them to the matching content.
  • Heading markup: the homepage sends two copies of its H1 for desktop/mobile layouts. Consolidate if practical; this is a lower priority than addresses and links.

Are the redirects reasonable?

Yes, the sampled redirects have clear purposes and no loop was found. HTTP upgrades to HTTPS (308); the bare domain goes to www (308); /growsearch and /growsearch/features move to the product subdomain (308); /Pricing becomes /pricing (308); /plans and /contact-us use temporary redirects (307); /blog/page/1 goes to /blog (308). The demo Shopify store’s root goes to /password (302). Prefer direct final URLs in navigation and sitemaps to avoid unnecessary hops.

What this audit cannot confirm

No Search Console or Analytics account access was used, so actual indexing, ranking, traffic, or account setup is unverified. Core Web Vitals and Lighthouse were not measured. Contact delivery, database persistence, demo email submission, preview generation, and signed-in flows were not tested. Structured data and mobile checks were limited to the pages described above.

Evidence and references

Source locations: next.config.ts (redirects and rewrites), src/app/layout.tsx (base metadata and Google tags), src/app/sitemap.ts and src/lib/site-pages.ts (sitemap entries), src/app/robots.ts (crawler rules), src/components/site/Footer.tsx (placeholders), src/components/site/nav-links.ts (menu destinations), src/lib/site-urls.ts (Shopify and demo URLs), src/app/affiliates/layout.tsx (noindex), src/app/blog/[slug]/page.tsx (article SEO).
